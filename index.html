AnyTone Flasher

Flash official firmware to AnyTone radios from your browser over USB.

This browser cannot access serial ports. Use a desktop version of Chrome, Edge or Opera (Web Serial API), served over HTTPS or from localhost.
Flashing erases the radio's codeplug and contacts. Read and save your codeplug with the CPS first. Use firmware made for your exact model and hardware version.
